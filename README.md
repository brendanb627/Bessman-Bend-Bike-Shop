## Overview
This software for Bend Bike Shop is intended to create an easy way for the bike shop to rent out bikes. It can be used at a kiosk or online and will also be integrated with the shop to ensure accurate inventory and similar features.

## User Scenarios
Marvin Mcbride was placing a bike reservation on his phone when it dies unexpectedly. Marvin goes into the store and asks to resume his reservation process with an employee at the counter. While its not a common occurrence, Marvin expects that the employee has the ability to pull up his unfinished reservation and help him wrap it up and place the bike reservation without having to start over on the kiosks.

Wheeley Tyred is an employee at Bend Bike Shop. A few bikes were recently delivered to the store and now need to be available for rent. Wheely needs to quickly register them so he can get back to helping customers. Wheely expects a simple process that he can quickly input into and select fields like the type of bike and possibly quantity if there are multiple of the same model. Wheeley also expects that the database and kiosks/website will update relatively quickly to show the new bikes.

Sarah Smith rented two hybrid bikes for a weekend trip. When she returns one bike on time but keeps the other for an extra hour, an employee needs to process the return. The employee expects the system to calculate and charge the appropriate fees for each bike separately, while allowing them to waive the late fee if necessary. Sarah expects to pay only for the rentals and applicable fees associated with each bike.

Liam Anderson is enjoying his trip and wants to keep his rental bike for another day. He calls Bend Bike Shop to request an extension. The employee expects to check future reservations and availability before extending the rental, ensuring the bike is not promised to another customer.

## Non-Goals
* Automated system to cancel rentals for unforeseen closure days
* Tracking for helmets
* Require an account
* Automated replacement charge for missing rentals
* Selling bikes through the system. Website is only for rentals
* No extending rentals via the website. You must talk to an employee
